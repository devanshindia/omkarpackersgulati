About Omkar Packers Gulati

India's Leading Supplier of Premium Transparent Plastic Packaging Boxes Since 1993.

Our Story

Omkar Packers Gulati was established in the year 1993 by Mr. Chander Mani Gulati.

For more than three decades, the company has been supplying premium quality transparent plastic packaging boxes to wholesalers, retailers and manufacturers throughout India.

Our products are widely used for Jewellery, Bangles, Medicines, Camphor, Pickles, Kesar, Belts, Gifts and numerous industrial packaging applications.

Today, Omkar Packers Gulati is recognised as one of India's leading suppliers of transparent plastic packaging products.
