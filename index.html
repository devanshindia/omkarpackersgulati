About Omkar Packers Gulati

Highest Quality Products Since 1993

Trusted For Over Three Decades

Established in 1993 by Mr. Chander Mani Gulati , Omkar Packers Gulati has become one of India's trusted suppliers of premium transparent plastic packaging boxes. Our commitment to quality, innovation and customer satisfaction has helped us serve wholesalers, retailers and manufacturers across India.

We specialize in manufacturing transparent plastic boxes for jewellery, bangles, medicines, pickles, dry fruits, camphor, cosmetics, saffron (kesar), food products and many other industries.
