About Partition Series

The Partition Series is one of Omkar Packers Gulati's most popular product lines. These compartment storage boxes are manufactured using premium quality transparent plastic and are ideal for storing multiple small items separately. They are widely used by jewellers, medical stores, hardware dealers, electronic industries and gift packaging businesses.
